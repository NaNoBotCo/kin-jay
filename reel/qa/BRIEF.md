# QA: the Kin Jay Facebook reel

Watch and listen to `../out/kin-jay-reel.mp4` (45 s, 1080×1920, Thai captions with English under them). It is a reel about the Thai Vegetarian Festival (กินเจ), 10–18 Oct 2026, for Facebook.

The soundtrack is synthesised: a lion-dance drum pattern at 120 bpm, cymbals, gong, a buzzer when garlic drops into the wok (~11.5 s), pops when flags land on the map (21.8–25.8 s), firecrackers (26.5–29.4 s), star pings (31.5–34.5 s).

Write `REPORT.txt` in this folder, plain text, one finding per line, each with a timestamp:
1. Audio: anything harsh, clipped, too loud or quiet against the rest, or out of sync with the picture (a hit that lands off its cut or stamp).
2. Picture: any caption that is cut off, overlapping, unreadable at phone size, or on screen too briefly to read (under ~1 s for a short line).
3. Thai: any caption that reads wrongly or unnaturally to a Thai speaker. Quote it and give a better wording.
4. Pace: the three weakest seconds, and what would make them more fun.
5. One line: would a scroller stop on the first 2 seconds? Why or why not.

Do not edit any file except REPORT.txt.
